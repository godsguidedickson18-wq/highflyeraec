We Believe Every Adult Deserves to Read Their Own Name

The story of Highflyer is not about classrooms and textbooks. It is about the stubborn, quiet belief that no human being should live their whole life locked out of the written word.

Our Story

Born From an Elderly Woman's Tears

In 2022, our founder Mr. Godsguidance Godknows Dickson sat beside a 68-year-old woman in a Port Harcourt hospital corridor. The doctor handed her a prescription and discharge form. She stared at it quietly, then passed it to her daughter — not because she couldn't see, but because she couldn't read a single word.

That moment broke Godsguidance's heart. The elderly woman was not unintelligent — she had raised six children, run a thriving market stall for thirty years, and memorised most of the Bible verses in Igbo. But no one had ever thought to teach her to read English.

Six months later, Highflyer opened in a single room behind Town Hall, 26 Rumuogholu Road, with three learners, two plastic chairs, and a handmade blackboard. Today, four years on, we have served more than 500 adults across Rivers State.

Our Mission

To equip illiterate and semi-literate adults in Rivers State with the reading, writing, and communication skills they need to live with full dignity and confidence — at fees that are honest, affordable, and clearly explained.

🌅

Our Vision

A Rivers State where no adult is turned away from opportunity, denied access to information, or made to feel ashamed simply because they were never given the chance to learn. Literacy is not a privilege. It is a right.

What We Stand For

The Values That Guide Every Class

🙏
Dignity Above All

Every learner enters as a full, capable adult who deserves respect. We never talk down to anyone or make anyone feel small for starting where they are starting.

🐢
Patient, Steady Progress

Learning as an adult takes longer and requires a kind of courage that children don't need. We move at your pace, not ours. There are no races here — only forward progress.

🤝
Community & Belonging

Our classes are communities. Learners become friends, encourage each other, and celebrate together. Many graduates say the friendships were as life-changing as the literacy itself.

⭐
Quality Without Compromise

Affordable does not mean second-rate. Our tutors are trained educators. Our approach is grounded in proven, phonics-based literacy methods designed specifically for adults.

🔒
Privacy & Confidentiality

Nothing that happens in our classrooms is shared outside them. Your enrolment, progress, and story belong to you alone — unless you choose to share them.

💡
Lifelong Learning

We believe learning has no age limit. Several graduates have gone on to earn WAEC equivalency certificates after leaving Highflyer.

The People Behind the Work

Our Leadership Team

Mr. Godsguidance Godknows Dickson
Founder & Executive Director

How Far We've Come

A Journey Built One Step at a Time

2022
Highflyer Opens Its Doors
Behind Town Hall, 26 Rumuogholu Road — 3 learners, 1 tutor, 2 plastic chairs, and a handmade blackboard.
2022
First Graduation Ceremony Held
The community hall was full. Half the room was in tears. Our first graduates received their Foundation Class certificates.
2023
Enrolment Grows to Over 80 Learners
Word spread through markets, churches, and community groups. Our second cohort launched and enrolment grew steadily.
2023
Builder's and Expression Classes Launched
Demand from Foundation graduates led to our Intermediate and Advanced levels. She read her name aloud at the ceremony and the crowd erupted.
2024
Remote Learning Support Introduced
Over 50 learners continued their studies via voice notes and printed worksheets — proving literacy learning can adapt to any circumstance.
2025
Partnerships with Churches, Markets & Community Groups
Highflyer deepened roots across Rivers State — partnering with local churches, market associations, and women's groups to refer and enrol adults.
2026
Website Launches — 500+ Lives Transformed
Our digital presence goes live, reaching new learners, volunteers, and sponsors across Nigeria and beyond.
