Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost? ▼
Our monthly fees are affordable and clearly explained when you enquire. Contact us and we will give you the full breakdown, privately and without pressure.
Am I too old to learn? ▼
Absolutely not. Our oldest learner was 78 when she enrolled. The brain never fully loses its ability to learn — it just needs the right teacher and the right environment.
Will other people know I'm here? ▼
No. Your enrolment is completely confidential. We never share names or details of any learner with anyone outside Highflyer. What happens at Highflyer stays at Highflyer.
Can I bring my baby or young child? ▼
Yes. Many of our female learners are mothers with no childcare options. You are welcome to bring a quiet child. We have a small play area at the Rumuogholu Centre. Please mention it when you enquire.
Do I receive a certificate when I finish? ▼
Yes! Every learner who completes a level receives an official Highflyer Adult Education Centre certificate. We hold a graduation ceremony at the end of each year where families are invited to celebrate together.
