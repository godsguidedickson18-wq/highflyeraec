10 Minutes a Day

Ten minutes of daily practice is worth more than two hours once a week. Play while waiting in a queue or before bed.

🔊

Say It Out Loud

When you see a letter or word, say it aloud — even when alone. Hearing yourself say it helps your brain remember it faster.

🤸

Wrong Answers Help

Nobody gets every answer right the first time. That is the whole point of practising. Be kind to yourself and keep going.
