What We Do

Three Skills That Change Everything

We start where you are. No shame. No rush. Steady, patient learning that fits around your life and your schedule.

📖

Reading

We begin with the alphabet, move through sounds, and work patiently until every word on a page becomes something you recognise. Relaxed, supportive, and designed so nobody ever feels embarrassed about where they start.

✍️

Writing

From forming your first letters to writing complete sentences, filling forms, and putting your own thoughts on paper — our writing classes give you a voice that goes beyond spoken words. Many learners write letters to family for the very first time.

🗣️

Communication

Reading and writing are only part of the story. We teach you to speak confidently — at the market, the hospital, in meetings, on the phone. Every person has something important to say. We help you say it well.

Our Three Class Levels

We Meet You Exactly Where You Are

Nobody starts in the wrong place here. Every level is honoured and every starting point welcomed.

Beginners

Foundation Class

For those who have never been to school or remember very little. Alphabet, basic sounds, and your very first words. Gentle, patient, completely at your pace.

Intermediate

Builder's Class

For those who know some letters and words but struggle with sentences. We build fluency, practical writing, and everyday tasks like reading forms and letters.

Advanced

Expression Class

For those who can read and write basic things but want to go much further — public speaking, formal letters, digital literacy, and professional communication.

Our Story

Born From a An elderly Tears

In 2022, our founder Mr. Godsguidance Godknows Dickson sat beside a 68-year-old woman in a Port Harcourt hospital corridor. The doctor handed her a prescription and discharge form. She stared at it quietly, then passed it to her daughter — not because she couldn't see, but because she couldn't read a single word.

That moment broke Godsguidance's heart. The elderly woman was not unintelligent — she had raised six children, run a thriving market stall for thirty years, and memorised the most of Bible verses in Igbo. But no one had ever thought to teach her to read English.

Six months later, Highflyer opened in a single room behind Town Hall, 26 Rumuogholu road, with three learners, two plastic chairs, and a handmade blackboard. Today, over six years on, we have served more than 500 adults across Rivers State.

Our Mission

To equip illiterate and semi-literate adults in Rivers State with the reading, writing, and communication skills they need to live with full dignity and confidence — at fees that are honest, affordable, and clearly explained.

🌅

Our Vision

A Rivers State where no adult is turned away from opportunity, denied access to information, or made to feel ashamed simply because they were never given the chance to learn. Literacy is not a privilege. It is a right.

Our Programmes

Start Where You Are. Go as Far as You Dream.

Not sure which level is right for you? Call us and we'll help decide — kindly and privately.

Level 1 — Beginners
Foundation Class
"The bravest thing you will ever do is walk through our door the first time."

For those who have never attended school, attended briefly and remember very little, or tried to learn before without the right support. We begin at the very beginning — and there is nothing wrong with that. Most learners say starting Foundation Class was the single best decision of their adult life.

⏱️ Duration: 6 Months
📅 Days: Mon & Wed or Tue & Thu
🕕 Morning: 8:00–10:00 AM
🌙 Evening: 4:00–6:00 PM
👥 Class Size: Max 12
💰 Fee: Affordable monthly — disclosed on enquiry

What You Will Learn

  • The 26 letters of the English alphabet — upper and lower case
  • Letter sounds and how they combine to form words (phonics)
  • Recognising your own name and common everyday words
  • Counting numbers 1–100 and basic number recognition
  • Reading simple signs: Exit, Danger, Men/Women, Open/Closed
  • Writing your name, address, and date of birth
  • Basic conversational phrases for everyday Nigerian situations

By the end of Foundation Class, you will read simple sentences, write basic personal information, and communicate in short, clear English sentences — ready and genuinely confident for Builder's Class.

Level 2 — Intermediate
Builder's Class
"You know the letters. Now let them become words. Let words become your power."

For learners who know some letters and words but struggle with full sentences, longer texts, or communicating clearly in English. Perhaps you attended school briefly as a child, or have been practising alone. We take what you already know and build on it — steadily and surely.

⏱️ Duration: 8 Months
📅 Days: Mon & Wed or Tue & Thu
🕕 Morning: 8:00–10:00 AM
🌙 Evening: 4:00–6:00 PM
👥 Class Size: Max 15
💰 Fee: Affordable monthly — disclosed on enquiry

What You Will Learn

  • Reading short paragraphs and understanding their meaning
  • Writing complete sentences and short paragraphs in your own words
  • Reading and completing common forms — hospital, bank, school
  • Understanding text messages, receipts, and official letters
  • Spelling common everyday words correctly and consistently
  • Telling the time, reading a calendar, and understanding important dates
  • Speaking in full sentences with correct grammar in everyday situations
  • Basic arithmetic — adding, subtracting, and managing personal money

By the end of Builder's Class, you will read a short newspaper article, write a complete paragraph, fill your own official documents without help, and hold a confident basic conversation with strangers and professionals.

Level 3 — Advanced
Expression Class
"You have learned the basics. Now use them to speak, write, and lead with confidence."

For adults who can read and write basic things but want to go much further. Perhaps you can write a short message but struggle with longer documents. Perhaps you feel nervous in formal settings — a government office, your child's school, a job interview. This class takes your literacy from functional to genuinely confident.

⏱️ Duration: 10 Months
📅 Days: Mon & Wed or Tue & Thu
🕕 Morning: 8:00–10:00 AM
🌙 Evening: 4:00–6:00 PM
👥 Class Size: Max 18
💰 Fee: Affordable monthly — disclosed on enquiry

What You Will Learn

  • Reading newspapers, government notices, contracts, and longer documents
  • Writing formal and informal letters — complaints, requests, applications
  • Effective oral communication — presenting ideas clearly in group settings
  • Professional phone conversations — confident and clear
  • Digital literacy basics — WhatsApp, using a smartphone effectively
  • Business writing — invoices, receipts, basic business correspondence
  • Public speaking and confidence-building exercises
  • Understanding your rights as a citizen, patient, and worker
  • Preparation for further education or vocational training

Graduates of Expression Class leave with full communication skills to navigate life confidently — advocating for themselves professionally, building businesses, and supporting their children's education. Several have earned WAEC equivalency certificates.

The First Word She Ever Read"MARKET"

"I spent 30 years selling pepper and tomatoes at Rumuola Market. Every day I walked past the sign that read RUMUOLA MARKET and thought it was just a shape — like a drawing. When I read it for the first time, I stood there for five minutes. People must have thought I was strange. I didn't care. I was reading my own market. I had been there 30 years and was finally seeing it for the very first time.

Now I keep a small notebook. Every day I write what I sold and how much money I made. My accounts are better than they have ever been. My daughter says I am now a proper businesswoman. I say — I always was. I just needed the tools."

When the pastor asked me to read the scripture for the whole congregation three months later, I stood up and read it from the Bible with my own eyes and my own voice. The congregation clapped. They thought I was just emotional. They don't know what that moment truly meant. Only I know."

I wrote: 'Dear daughter. I am writing this myself. I learned how at Highflyer. I am proud of myself and I want you to be proud too. Your mother.'

She called when she received it, crying so hard she couldn't speak for three minutes. When she finally spoke she said — 'Mama, I framed it. I put it on my wall.' That letter is on her wall in Lagos right now. I wrote it. Me. At 67 years old."

My children are in school now. When they come home with homework, I sit with them and read it. Not do it for them — that is their job. But I can sit with them and read it. That was all I ever wanted."

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost? ▼
Our monthly fees are affordable and clearly explained when you enquire. We believe every learner deserves to know what they are committing to before they sign up. Contact us and we will give you the full breakdown, privately and without pressure.
Can I get help with the fee? ▼
Yes. We have a sponsorship programme supported by individuals, churches, businesses, and community partners who pay the tuition of learners who cannot afford it. Tell us honestly when you enquire — your information is completely private and treated with full respect.
Am I too old to learn? ▼
Absolutely not. Our oldest learner was 78 when she enrolled. The brain never fully loses its ability to learn — it just needs the right teacher and the right environment. We have taught people of every age.
Will other people know I'm here? ▼
No. Your enrolment is completely confidential. We never share names or details of any learner with anyone outside Highflyer — not employers, not family, not community leaders. What happens at Highflyer stays at Highflyer.
Can I bring my baby or young child? ▼
Yes. Many of our female learners are mothers with no childcare options. You are welcome to bring a quiet child. We have a small play area at the Rumuogholu Centre. Please mention it when you enquire so we can prepare.
Do I receive a certificate when I finish? ▼
Yes! Every learner who completes a level receives an official Highflyer Adult Education Centre certificate. We hold a graduation ceremony at the end of each year where families are invited to celebrate together. It is one of the most joyful events you will ever attend.
