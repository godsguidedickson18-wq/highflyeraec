This Work Belongs to All of Us

Highflyer exists because ordinary people decided that adult illiteracy was not someone else's problem. Whether you give your time, skills, or resources — you change real lives.

Ways to Get Involved

Find Your Role in This Story

🧑‍🏫
Volunteer as a Tutor
"You don't have to be a teacher. You just have to care."

Our tutors are the heart of everything we do. Many are not professional teachers — they are bankers, nurses, students, and retirees who give a few hours each week. We provide full training, all materials, and ongoing support.

  • As little as 2 hours per week
  • Full free tutor training provided (2-day workshop)
  • All lesson plans and materials provided
  • Official reference letter after 6 months of service
Apply to Volunteer →
🏢
Sponsor a Learner
"Pay one person's monthly tuition. Change one life. Completely."

Through our sponsorship programme, individuals, businesses, churches, and community groups pay the monthly tuition of a learner who cannot afford it. You give them the gift of literacy. That is not a small thing.

  • Pay a learner's monthly tuition directly
  • Corporate CSR partnership packages available
  • Named sponsorship recognition if desired
  • Regular updates on your sponsored learner's progress
Enquire About Sponsorship →
🤲
Community Partnership
"Help us reach the people who need us most."

Churches, mosques, women's associations, market cooperatives, and community groups are some of our most powerful partners. If you know adults who struggle to read, connect them with us — we'll do everything else. We also come to your group for outreach talks.

  • Free outreach talks and awareness sessions
  • Enrolment materials provided at no cost
  • We come to your meeting, service, or event
  • No cost and no paperwork — just community
