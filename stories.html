The Words That Changed Their Lives

👩🏿
Mrs. Ngozi Amaechi
Age 54 · Trader, Rumuola Market · Graduated 2023
Foundation → Builder's
The First Word She Ever Read"MARKET"

"I spent 30 years selling pepper and tomatoes at Rumuola Market. Every day I walked past the sign that read RUMUOLA MARKET and thought it was just a shape — like a drawing. When I read it for the first time, I stood there for five minutes. I was reading my own market. I had been there 30 years and was finally seeing it for the very first time.

Now I keep a small notebook. Every day I write what I sold and how much money I made. My accounts are better than they have ever been. My daughter says I am now a proper businesswoman. I say — I always was. I just needed the tools."

👨🏿
Mr. Emmanuel Dike
Age 61 · Church Deacon · Intermediate Level 2024
Builder's Class
The First Thing He Ever Read AloudPsalm 23:1

"I have been a deacon at my church for 22 years. Every Sunday I would say 'Amen' loudly so nobody would know I was not reading along. For 22 years I did this.

When I finally read Psalm 23 aloud at Highflyer — just with my tutor — I wept. Not sad tears. Something else entirely. Like a wall around my heart for my whole life had finally come down.

When the pastor asked me to read the scripture for the whole congregation three months later, I stood up and read it from the Bible with my own eyes and my own voice. The congregation clapped. They don't know what that moment truly meant. Only I know."

👩🏿‍🦳
Mama Chidinma Okafor
Age 67 · Retired · All Three Levels 2024
Foundation → Expression
The First Letter She Ever Wrote"Dear Daughter..."

"My daughter lives in Lagos. For forty years, whenever she called I would talk — but I could never write to her. When I finished Expression Class, the first thing I did was write her a letter. In my own hand. In my own words.

I wrote: 'Dear daughter. I am writing this myself. I learned how at Highflyer. I am proud of myself and I want you to be proud too. Your mother.'

She called when she received it, crying so hard she couldn't speak for three minutes. When she finally spoke she said — 'Mama, I framed it. I put it on my wall.' That letter is on her wall in Lagos right now. I wrote it. Me. At 67 years old."

👨🏿‍🦲
Mr. Sunday Nwachukwu
Age 45 · Bricklayer · Foundation Class 2024
Foundation Class
The First Sign He Ever Read"DANGER"

"I work on construction sites. There are warning signs everywhere. For twenty years, I had to wait for someone to tell me what they said.

When I read the word DANGER on my own for the first time, I didn't feel proud. I felt safe. For the first time in twenty years on a building site, I felt genuinely safe.

My children are in school now. When they come home with homework, I sit with them and read it. Not do it for them — that is their job. But I can sit with them and read it. That was all I ever wanted."
